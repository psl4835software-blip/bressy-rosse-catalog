# Public catalog and administration

## Scope

Public catalog home, brand and category browsing, discounts, and a protected administration area for maintaining products, images, and promotions.

## Visitor mode

Experience for shoppers; Operate for authorized catalog managers. The approved visual world is shared, while the admin experience favors task clarity.

## Audience and task

Shoppers in Bolivia browse BR or Bressy Rosse and inspect products and prices in bolivianos. Authorized staff sign in, create or edit products, upload images, assign the correct brand and category, and mark promotion items. Public checkout and payments are out of scope for this phase.

## Content and proof

Real product names, descriptions, photographs, prices, discount dates, and brand assets must come from the user or authorized staff. Do not invent stock, delivery, customer, or product claims.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One shared catalog opens two distinct retail vitrines, BR and Bressy Rosse, with each product and price easy to identify. It refuses the anonymous equal-card storefront.

**OWN-WORLD:** A contemporary shop sample cabinet carries the page: warm paper, deep ink framing, rose for Bressy Rosse, restrained brass label details, and clean glass-like display openings. Navigation and category labels behave as real controls; material never obscures names or prices. Honest risk: too much cabinet decoration could make browsing feel like a museum, so the product information stays dominant.

**STORY:** The visitor chooses a brand, then a category, then browses published products without a checkout. The signature interaction is the two-brand vitrine selector, which switches the visible catalog and its category labels. A separate `/admin` area lets authorized staff maintain products, images, and discount pricing; its table and forms inherit the material system with less decoration so frequent tasks stay quick. The same catalog repository boundary can later connect to the existing system.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A narrow ink header holds the wordmark and brand navigation. The page opens on one broad framed vitrine with two unequal brand apertures and the "Descuentos" link on its lower rail; category controls sit directly below, followed by the live catalog area. Product prices in Bs and the discount action remain visible without opening a drawer. On phones, the vitrine becomes a single brand switcher and a horizontal category rail, with no hover-only controls.

## Constraints and open decisions

- Brand mapping is BR (school supplies, toys, kitchen articles, jewelry) and Bressy Rosse (cosmetics).
- Market and display currency: Bolivia, Bs.
- No checkout, payment flow, or inventory synchronization in this phase.
- The existing system's API, hosting target, Supabase project credentials, product inventory, images, and campaign dates have not been supplied yet.
- The first administrative account must be provisioned without committing credentials to the repository.
