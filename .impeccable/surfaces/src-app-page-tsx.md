# Public catalog and administration

## Scope

Public catalog home, brand and category browsing, discounts, and a protected administration area for maintaining products, images, and promotions.

## Visitor mode

Experience for shoppers; Operate for authorized catalog managers. The public storefront carries the expressive visual world; admin screens inherit its color, typography, and rounded controls while keeping frequent tasks direct.

## Audience and task

Shoppers in Bolivia browse BR or Bressy Rosse and inspect products and prices in bolivianos. Authorized staff sign in, create or edit products, upload images, assign the correct brand and category, and mark promotion items. Public checkout and payments are out of scope.

## Content and proof

Real product names, descriptions, photographs, prices, discount dates, and brand assets must come from the user or authorized staff. Do not invent products, product imagery, stock, delivery, customer, or product claims.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A bright, fluid digital showroom makes both brands feel contemporary and easy to explore; the page should feel airy, tactile, and rounded rather than built from square display cases.

**OWN-WORLD:** Liquid glass belongs on the floating main navigation and the two in-context brand launchers, where translucency separates useful controls from the luminous berry background. The rest of the storefront stays clear and readable: pale pearl surfaces, deep plum ink, a restrained rose accent, softly lit color fields, rounded image wells, and pill-shaped category controls. Glass is a material for these layered controls, not a blur applied indiscriminately.

**STORY:** Shoppers choose Bressy Rosse cosmetics or BR (school supplies, toys, kitchen articles, and jewelry), then browse published products and current discounts. Prices remain in Bs and the public experience has no checkout. Staff continue maintaining real catalog content through the protected admin area.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact floating glass header carries both brand links and discounts. A spacious berry gradient hero introduces the two brand catalogs, with short direct calls to action and two overlapping translucent brand launchers as the visual signature. Categories follow as a horizontal pill rail. Nothing suggests that placeholder imagery or sample products are real.

**FORM:** The user rejected the previous square cabinet proposal and explicitly requested a modern liquid-glass direction, especially circular or pill-shaped buttons. This direction supersedes the previous concept.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with rendered desktop/mobile verification, one detector pass, the finish verdict, and DESIGN.md documentation.

## Constraints and open decisions

- Brand mapping is BR (school supplies, toys, kitchen articles, jewelry) and Bressy Rosse (cosmetics).
- Market and display currency: Bolivia, Bs.
- No checkout, payment flow, or inventory synchronization in this phase.
- No product catalog, product photos, logo files, testimonials, or product claims have been supplied; use the real catalog and mark missing imagery clearly.
- The first administrative account must be provisioned without committing credentials to the repository.
